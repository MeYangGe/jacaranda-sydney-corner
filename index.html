The Corner House · Jacaranda Season

On a quiet street corner, a cream terrace keeps company with the purple rain of jacaranda season.

Three storeys of curved brick and iron lace, two purple benches, and canopies that rise low at the front and high at the back — no two trees alike. By night the lamps come on through the branches, and the whole corner glows, quietly.
